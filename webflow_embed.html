FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

Why is the Cognition Test paid?
The Cognition Test requires significant infrastructure to administer fairly — question banks across multiple domains, automated marking, and identity verification. The fee covers these operational costs and signals genuine intent from applicants.
How do payments work?
Payments are made bi-weekly in USD via Stripe or PayPal. You'll receive a full breakdown of completed tasks and earnings in your dashboard before each payout.
Do I need coding skills to join?
No — coding is just one of four tiers. Basic covers generalist work, STEM covers sciences, and Professional covers finance and accounting. Choose the tier that matches your skills.
What happens if I don't pass the test?
You can retake the free practice test anytime. If you don't pass the Cognition Test, you'll receive detailed feedback and can reapply after a 30-day period.
How long does the qualification last?
Your qualification is valid for 12 months from the date you pass. A short re-qualification is required annually to confirm your skills remain current.
When can I start working?
Once you pass the Cognition Test you gain access to your contributor dashboard where available projects are listed by tier. You can start applying immediately after qualification.
